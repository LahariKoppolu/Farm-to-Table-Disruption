# 🌾 Farm-to-Table Disruptions – Business Intelligence Project

This project analyzes the potential economic impact of 2025 U.S. immigration policy changes on agricultural labor markets, crop yields, and food prices. It combines predictive analytics and business intelligence techniques to simulate multiple policy scenarios and provide stakeholders with visual decision-making tools.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

As part of an academic research initiative at Pace University, this project modeled labor supply disruptions, economic losses, and mitigation strategies in U.S. agriculture. Using historical data and scenario modeling, it forecasts how immigration changes might affect the farm-to-table supply chain — particularly through workforce shortages.
